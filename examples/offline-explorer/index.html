Offline-first explorer · airline accidents 2006–2007

The same data three ways: queried on the cluster, and copied into two PouchDB "devices" in this browser that sync with different cluster nodes. The online switches are simulated: a device stops syncing, the remote pane refuses to send requests. To try it for real, stop the douchdb nodes or use the browser's offline mode.
